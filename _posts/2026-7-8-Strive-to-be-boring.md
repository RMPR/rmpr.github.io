title: Strive to be boring
tags: [software-engineering, career]
---

Like every software engineer, I am funneling my inner woodworker. And ever since
I started I can't help but notice that those tools are boring. In the good
sense, they get out of the way. When people don't have to think about what you
have built then you have achieved something remarkable.

I am not advocating for everything to be boring but there are stuff like
Operating Systems (I hate you Windows), Text editors, etc. That _need_ to be
boring and from that boring base anybody can build something else. When I see
Software changing under my feet for no good reason, it irks me.

In short when you are building foundational things: get out of your way to get
out of the way.
