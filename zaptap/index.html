          How to play

          • Each day brings five places, the same for everyone. Find them on the globe.
          • Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom. Press and hold on a spot until the ring fills up to lock in your guess.
          • You get 100 points for landing within 22 km. Points drop with distance, down to 0 at 16,250 km.
          • Later rounds are harder and count more: ×1 ×1 ×2 ×3 ×3, for 1000 points max.
          • Daily bonus: a pin in the right country scores at least 25, and the right continent scores at least 10.
          • A new ZapTap starts at midnight Oslo time, and so does the leaderboard.
          • Your daily score goes on the team leaderboard under your name. There are achievements to unlock too.
